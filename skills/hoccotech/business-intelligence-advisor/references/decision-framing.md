# Decision framing

- Format: Decision | Owner | Options | Evidence needed | Deadline | Reversibility.
- Baselines: prior period, same period last year, peer segment, target. Report absolute and relative change.
- Diagnose by splitting: time, channel, product/service, location, customer segment, new vs returning.
- Guardrails: sample size, seasonality, outliers, definition changes, double counting, survivorship.
- Output: finding, evidence, interpretation, recommendation, confidence (high/medium/low), what would change the conclusion.
