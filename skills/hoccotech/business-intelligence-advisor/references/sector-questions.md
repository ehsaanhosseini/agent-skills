# Sector questions

- **PMS/hotels:** occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, lead time, cancellation and no-show rate, channel mix and commission, length of stay, rate vs pickup pace.
- **Marketplace:** listing supply and quality, search-to-contact conversion, response time, time to sell, take rate, fraud rate, buyer/seller retention.
- **Clinics/hospitals:** wait time, no-show rate, utilization of rooms and staff, throughput, claim rejection and days in receivables. Aggregate only.
- **Salons:** chair/staff utilization, rebooking rate, no-show and late-cancel rate, average ticket, product attach rate, package redemption.
- **Studio ops:** utilization, project margin, scope change rate, approval cycle time, on-time delivery, receivables age.
