# Domain patterns (public-safe, generic)

Use as prompts for discovery, not as requirements. Confirm every rule with the owner.

- **Property management (PMS, hotels):** inventory by room type and date, rate plans, reservation lifecycle (inquiry, hold, confirmed, checked-in, checked-out, cancelled, no-show), overbooking and channel sync, folio/charges, housekeeping status, night audit, multi-currency and tax.
- **Marketplace and mobile commerce (vehicles, goods):** seller/buyer/admin roles, listing lifecycle and moderation, search and filters, offers and messaging, fraud and spam, fees and payouts, deep links and push notifications, trust and reviews.
- **Local/regional consumer platforms:** locale, language and direction (RTL), local payment and messaging channels, unreliable connectivity, cost-sensitive infrastructure.
- **Studio operations:** projects, briefs, approvals, deliverables, time and capacity, client portal, invoicing, asset versioning.
- **Clinics and hospitals:** patient identity, appointments and queues, encounters, orders and results, billing/insurance, consent, strict role access, break-glass access, full audit trail, retention. Treat health data as regulated; involve compliance before design is final. Not medical advice.
- **Beauty salons:** services and durations, staff skills and schedules, resource conflicts, deposits, no-shows, packages and memberships, product stock, commissions, reminders.
- **Cross-cutting:** roles/permissions, audit log, notifications, reporting, imports/exports, integrations, data retention, backups.
