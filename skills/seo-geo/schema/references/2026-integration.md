# Integration review: consumer-aware vocabulary validation

Separate Schema.org validity from current search-feature eligibility. Validate markup against visible truthful page content and current official consumer documentation. Do not add SearchAction to promise the retired Google sitelinks search box. Verify the present eligibility of FAQ/HowTo and other rich-result features before recommending them; do not copy historical eligibility claims. Rich Results Test non-detection of an unsupported type is not by itself invalid vocabulary.

## Verification

Work from authorized inputs; record observations, assumptions, unverified evidence and the actual checks performed. Compare the proposed outcome with the baseline and preserve unrelated approved behavior. Do not label recommendations or metadata validation as observed product results.
