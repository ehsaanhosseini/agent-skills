# Integration review: measurement and answer accuracy

Maintain a small versioned prompt portfolio across buyer tasks, locales and stages; record engine, date, visible sources and repeated observations. Separate citation, recommendation, answer accuracy and downstream conversion. A cited page is not necessarily a recommended brand; model outputs vary and cannot establish ranking-factor causality. Correct public factual errors through accurate source material, not prompt injection or fabricated consensus. No special schema, llms.txt or fixed answer format guarantees inclusion in Google AI features.

## Verification

Work from authorized inputs; record observations, assumptions, unverified evidence and the actual checks performed. Compare the proposed outcome with the baseline and preserve unrelated approved behavior. Do not label recommendations or metadata validation as observed product results.
