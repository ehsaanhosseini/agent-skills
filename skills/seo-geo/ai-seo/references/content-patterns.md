# Human-first answer patterns

Choose the form that best answers the reader's question; no fixed passage length is an established AI ranking rule.

## Definition

Name the concept, explain it in plain terms, give a concrete example and define relevant limits. Cite factual claims where support is needed.

## Process

State prerequisites, ordered actions, expected outcomes and recovery for likely failures. Do not omit security or domain constraints to make the answer shorter.

## Comparison

Compare equivalent plans or use cases with current dated evidence. Explain tradeoffs and who each option suits; do not fabricate missing capabilities or rank the publisher first by default.

## Questions and proof

Answer real customer questions with visible, accurate information. Use tables and FAQs when they help comprehension, not to manufacture markup. Include original evidence and explicit assumptions; never invent statistics or expert quotes.

Verify readability, semantic HTML, content accuracy and relevant accessibility. Structured data must match the visible page and current consumer requirements. It does not guarantee citation or rich-result appearance.
