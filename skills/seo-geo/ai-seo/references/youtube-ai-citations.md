# Video sourceability

Use accurate titles, descriptions, chapters, captions and transcripts to help people understand and verify video content. Keep product claims, dates, examples and linked sources consistent with the actual recording. Review captions manually when names, prices or technical details matter.

For a demonstration, use synthetic data and distinguish conceptual footage from shipped behavior. Preserve rights to music, voices, imagery and source clips; no fabricated testimonials. Provide an accessible written explanation when the video alone cannot communicate the task.
