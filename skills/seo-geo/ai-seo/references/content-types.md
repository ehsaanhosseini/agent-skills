# Match content to the audience task

Category pages should explain the problem, solution class, suitability and real limitations. Product pages should accurately state capabilities, prerequisites, integrations and commercial terms. How-to pages should help the reader complete a real task with tested examples. Comparison pages should compare equivalent alternatives using evidence and current dates.

For marketplaces and commerce, show real product/inventory facts, price and fulfillment conditions rather than generating empty combinations. For healthcare and regulated services, require appropriate subject expertise and avoid personalized or unsupported claims. For local services, represent actual eligibility and coverage; no fabricated locations.

Preserve distinct locale needs and approved canonical/hreflang strategy. Choose a content family based on customer research and actual information gaps, then use content-strategy or site-architecture only for the planning work needed. No universal content type guarantees AI recommendations.
