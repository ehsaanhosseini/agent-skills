# Observing format changes

Maintain a versioned sample of real audience questions across category learning, evaluation, pricing, implementation and support. Record wording, date, engine/mode, locale, visible answer/source evidence and uncertainty; repeat under comparable conditions.

If source formats change, inspect whether query mix, geography, personalization, model mode or content availability also changed. A small sample does not prove an engine-wide update or justify rebuilding all content. Preserve useful reader-facing formats and test a bounded hypothesis.

Use only officially exposed or otherwise authorized evidence; do not rely on hidden internal APIs or undocumented developer-console extraction. Review costs/privacy before adopting monitoring services and do not create recurring checks unless requested.
