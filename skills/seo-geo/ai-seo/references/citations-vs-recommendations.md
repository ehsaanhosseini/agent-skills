# Citation, mention and recommendation

Track separately whether a page was retrieved (only if observable), cited, the brand mentioned, or the product recommended for a specific need. Record favorable, neutral, conditional and negative framing along with source, engine/mode, locale and date. Do not infer hidden retrieval from an uncited answer.

A citation may support category information while the answer recommends a competitor. Third-party reviews, accurate independent coverage and original owned content can all be useful to readers, but their causal effect on model recommendations is not established by a single answer or correlation study.

For a comparison page, assess factual usefulness and qualified conversions alongside visible citations. Maintain accurate first-party product/pricing facts and correct demonstrably false public information through approved channels. No fabricated consensus, fake reviews, paid ranking links or mass third-party publishing.

Compare repeated observations using a stable question set and note sampling limits. If monitoring data is unavailable, deliver a measurement plan rather than claiming share of voice.
