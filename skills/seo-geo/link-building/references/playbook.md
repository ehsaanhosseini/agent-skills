# Link Building playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience relevance, existing useful assets, source quality, editorial context, outreach capacity and risk tolerance.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose links that help readers discover a useful resource. Prioritize original research, practical tools, unlinked legitimate mentions and editorial contributions. Assess topical relevance, real readership and placement integrity rather than proprietary authority scores alone. Disclose sponsorships and qualify paid links under current search guidance.

## Worked example

Offer a documented booking-operations resource to an industry publication because it answers a reader need; do not purchase exact-match links or trade links at scale.

## Verify and diagnose

Verify real placement and context, correct destination, appropriate rel attributes and referral quality. Record earned versus sponsored placements and assess results without causal ranking claims.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
