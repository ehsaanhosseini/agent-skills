# Sms playbook

## Establish the decision

Jurisdictions, message purpose, channel, consent records, recipient timezone, frequency, provider and unsubscribe handling.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Separate transactional from promotional purpose and verify current regional/carrier/platform requirements before launch. Specify explicit opt-in evidence, sender identity, quiet-hour handling, frequency limits and opt-out processing. Map triggers, exits and cross-channel suppression before drafting short messages. Treat WhatsApp template/session constraints as version-sensitive.

## Worked example

An appointment reminder should reveal only necessary scheduling details; a promotional follow-up requires its own valid basis and must stop promptly on opt-out. Do not include diagnoses or sensitive appointment reasons.

## Verify and diagnose

Use sandbox/test recipients for approved implementation. Verify opt-in, opt-out variants, delivery errors, duplicate triggers, timezone boundaries and suppression propagation. Measure completed intended actions and complaints.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
