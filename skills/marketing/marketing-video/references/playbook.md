# Marketing Video playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience action, approved claims, duration, placement, reference rights, actual product flow, assets and production constraints.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose screen recording, filmed demonstration, animation or generated footage by what must be proved. Write a beat sheet linking hook, problem, demonstration, evidence and action. For reference edits, describe timing/composition rather than copying protected assets. Record only synthetic or approved data and plan captions, voiceover rights and accessible delivery.

## Worked example

For a PMS demo, record a sample reservation moving through a documented workflow, including realistic loading or error handling; do not stage nonexistent functionality as live product.

## Verify and diagnose

Review the rendered video for factual/UI fidelity, audio levels, captions, pacing, licensed media, safe areas and final CTA. Distinguish scripts/storyboards from produced footage and test delivery playback.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
