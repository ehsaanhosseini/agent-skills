# Marketing Psychology playbook

## Establish the decision

Observed behavior, friction, audience context, candidate mechanism, evidence and user welfare risks.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Identify whether motivation, ability or prompting is limiting action before choosing an intervention. Use cognitive-load reduction, truthful social proof, clear defaults and honest loss/risk explanations as hypotheses. Show the mechanism and a plausible alternative explanation. Treat named models as lenses, not guaranteed causal laws or universal lift percentages.

## Worked example

A complex pricing page may benefit from a clearer comparison rather than artificial scarcity; test comprehension and appropriate plan selection as well as conversion.

## Verify and diagnose

Define measurable behavior, guardrails and falsification. Check accessibility, comprehension, cancellation/refund outcomes and unintended pressure; validate with user research or experiments.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
