# Social playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience, platform, goal, approved voice, original evidence, publishing capacity and existing content.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose a few content pillars tied to audience jobs, then match format to the idea. Build hooks that deliver the promised information and adapt source material to each platform instead of duplicating it blindly. For listening, score public mentions by relevance and urgency; prepare draft responses with context and disclosure.

## Worked example

Turn a product workflow into a LinkedIn carousel with problem, steps, evidence and a useful next action; do not invent customer stories or manufacture controversy.

## Verify and diagnose

Check claims, asset rights, accessibility/captions, current platform limits, destination links and brand approval. Evaluate meaningful conversations and qualified outcomes rather than reach alone.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
