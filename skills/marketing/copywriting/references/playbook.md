# Copywriting playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience job, awareness stage, offer, differentiators, approved proof, objections, page goal and voice.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose the page's promise and one primary action before writing. Build a message hierarchy from customer language, explaining benefits through concrete mechanisms and proof. Match objections with evidence near the relevant claim. Offer a few genuinely distinct directions with tradeoffs instead of many superficial variations.

## Worked example

For a dashboard product, connect consolidated reporting to a specific operational decision; mark unsupported time-saving numbers as evidence needed rather than fabricating a testimonial.

## Verify and diagnose

Check factual support, consistency with product behavior, accessible link labels, CTA destination, readability and mobile hierarchy. Separate draft choices from owner-approved messaging.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
