# Onboarding playbook

## Establish the decision

User job, activation definition, product prerequisites, funnel by cohort, permissions and initial empty states.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Inventory steps from account creation to a meaningful outcome. Remove or defer steps not needed for that outcome, while preserving security and domain prerequisites. Choose product-first, guided setup or realistic demo data according to complexity. Make progress truthful, optional guidance dismissible and recovery paths clear. Validate activation against subsequent retained use rather than assuming checklist completion is value.

## Worked example

For a PMS, first value might be a verified sample reservation or safely imported availability, not inviting staff before the property exists. Distinguish sandbox demonstration from live reservations.

## Verify and diagnose

Test fresh, returning, interrupted, failed-import, empty and permission-limited sessions. Measure activation rate and time by eligible cohort, plus later retained outcomes and support burden.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
