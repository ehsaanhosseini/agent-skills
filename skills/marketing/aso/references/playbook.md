# Aso playbook

## Establish the decision

Store URL, locale, category, public listing, authorized console exports, install funnel and business goal.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Separate public observations from hidden keywords and console-only metrics. Compare relevant peers in the same locale and category. Audit title/subtitle or short description, value proposition, screenshot sequence, accessibility, release notes and disclosure accuracy. Propose keyword and asset hypotheses grounded in the app's real capabilities. Verify current Apple/Google field constraints before drafting counted alternatives.

## Worked example

For a booking app, storyboard screenshots around finding availability, booking, and managing a reservation. If images or console data cannot be inspected, label those dimensions unverified rather than scoring them as poor.

## Verify and diagnose

Count characters or bytes according to official field rules. Track listing impressions, product-page visits, installs and retention by locale/source; use store experiments when available. Report rubric scores as subjective diagnostics, never ranking predictions.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
