# Paywalls playbook

## Establish the decision

Entitlements, free/paid value, trigger, billing platform, trial/renewal terms, audience and purchase funnel.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Place prompts at a real value or limit moment; explain what is already available and what payment unlocks. Compare trial, metered access or feature gating against cost and user value. Make price, period, renewal and dismissal clear; preserve access to paid entitlements. On mobile, check current store purchase and restore requirements.

## Worked example

A usage-limit prompt should show actual usage and reset timing; a customer who already paid should see restore/support rather than another sales prompt.

## Verify and diagnose

Test free, trial, paid, expired, offline, pending purchase, failed payment, restored purchase and dismissal paths. Measure qualified upgrade, refunds, retained revenue and support complaints.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
