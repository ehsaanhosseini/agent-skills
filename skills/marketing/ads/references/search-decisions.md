# Search campaign decisions

Establish buyer/product, geography, budget cap, contribution margin or allowable CAC, qualification criteria, sales cycle and conversion lag. Mark missing inputs MISSING; do not guess spend or expected ROAS. Reconcile cheap leads with qualified opportunities and retained value.

Map intent -> theme/keyword -> negatives -> ad promise -> landing task -> confirmed conversion -> qualification feedback. Separate brand/non-brand when useful and keep structure proportional to traffic. For hotel PMS software exclude irrelevant room-booking/job intent without blocking valid adjacent buyer queries.

For each search-term export, record query, theme, spend, clicks, confirmed/qualified outcomes and observation window. Classify irrelevant, relevant/unproven and relevant/productive. Insufficient data does not prove failure. Check conversion lag, missing offline outcomes and low volume before pausing a term. No identity upload is implied.

Choose bidding by current platform capabilities, reliable outcome volume, lag, business goal and control needs. No universal conversion threshold or automatic winner among manual/maximize/target strategies is prescribed. Define learning window, loss cap and review point; avoid confounding simultaneous changes. Platform forecasts/recommendations are inputs, not guarantees or permission.

For B2B compare qualified CPA and payback using actual cohorts. If downstream data is unavailable, call the outcome a proxy and state how to validate it. Check current ad/remarketing/sensitive-category policies before audience choices.

Deliver a draft campaign matrix, explicit exclusions, landing alignment, signal-readiness findings and stop/continue criteria, ending with facts, hypotheses and missing evidence. No spend or account edits implied.
