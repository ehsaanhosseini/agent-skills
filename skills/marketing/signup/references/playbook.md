# Signup playbook

## Establish the decision

Signup audience, required fields, authentication methods, validation, verification obligations, devices and funnel failures.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Separate legally/security-required inputs from optional profiling. Choose one-step or progressive registration by actual complexity. Specify field labels, validation, password/SSO states, duplicate-account handling, recovery and clear terms. Preserve anti-abuse, accessibility and verified-identity requirements; friction reduction cannot justify weakening them.

## Worked example

Defer optional company-size questions until after account creation if allowed, but keep required age/consent/verification checks and avoid revealing whether arbitrary emails have accounts.

## Verify and diagnose

Test happy path, invalid input, existing account, expired verification, SSO failure, retries, keyboard and mobile. Measure eligible completion and downstream activation plus fraud/support guardrails.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
