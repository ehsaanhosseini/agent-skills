# Competitors playbook

## Establish the decision

Approved positioning, comparison audience, current product facts, competitor primary sources and target page intent.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose single-alternative, alternatives list, direct versus or comparison matrix to match reader intent. Compare the same plans, dates and use cases. Include strengths, constraints, switching costs and who should choose each option. Use original product evidence and maintain a claim/source/date ledger; do not invent missing feature gaps.

## Worked example

For a PMS versus spreadsheet page, explain when a spreadsheet is adequate and when multi-user controls matter; cite actual capabilities and migration effort.

## Verify and diagnose

Check claims against current sources, broken links, accessibility, approved brand language and a single useful CTA. Monitor qualified conversion and review stale claims on product changes.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
