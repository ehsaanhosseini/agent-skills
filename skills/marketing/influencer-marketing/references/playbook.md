# Influencer Marketing playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience, creator evidence, objective, budget, usage rights, regulated claims and disclosure requirements.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Assess audience relevance, engagement quality and creator trust rather than followers alone. Distinguish paid reach, reusable creative and affiliate sales as different objectives. Specify deliverables, honest claims, review process, licensing, exclusivity and attribution. Start with a bounded pilot and inspect audience quality before scaling.

## Worked example

A beauty-booking product can commission a real workflow demo with clear sponsorship disclosure; do not script a creator to claim results they never experienced.

## Verify and diagnose

Review creative against the brief and current platform/ad disclosure rules. Measure qualified visits and contribution after fees/usage rights; compare against a baseline and inspect suspicious engagement.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
