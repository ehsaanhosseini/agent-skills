# Launch playbook

## Establish the decision

Release state, target segment, dependencies, support capacity, message approval, rollout constraints and success criteria.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose a launch size proportional to product readiness. Sequence internal rehearsal, controlled audience, broader communication and follow-up. Separate product deployment approval from campaign approval. Prepare factual release notes, demos, support answers and a fallback if adoption or reliability fails.

## Worked example

For a marketplace launch, validate supply availability before driving demand; a waitlist announcement must not imply live inventory.

## Verify and diagnose

Verify destinations, signup paths, tracking authorization, support ownership, claims, staged messages and rollback/escalation triggers. Measure activation and retained use alongside initial attention.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
