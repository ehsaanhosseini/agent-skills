# Referrals playbook

## Establish the decision

Advocacy moment, audience, margin, incentive budget, attribution window, eligibility, fraud risk and platform rules.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Distinguish customer referral from commercial affiliate recruitment and review solicitation. Give both sides understandable value and disclose incentives. Define qualifying events, refund/clawback treatment, self-referral handling, attribution conflict resolution and payout review. Request honest reviews without selecting only satisfied customers.

## Worked example

A booking SaaS can reward a referred account after a paid retention milestone; do not pay for positive reviews or release rewards for refunded/self-referred purchases.

## Verify and diagnose

Test duplicate referrals, attribution expiry, refund reversal and payout eligibility. Monitor incremental qualified customers, fraud, cost and retained margin; check current review/affiliate disclosure policies.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
