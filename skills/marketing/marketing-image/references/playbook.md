# Marketing Image playbook

## Establish the decision

Placement, required dimensions, brand assets, reference rights, accessibility, product fidelity and available tool permissions.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose real product captures, designed graphics or generated illustration by the communication goal. Define composition, focal point, copy-safe area and visual proof before choosing a tool. Preserve accurate product UI and label conceptual mockups. Prepare variations for aspect ratios without automatic cropping of essential information.

## Worked example

A booking-product hero can show a synthetic-data screen with a clear workflow; do not fabricate recognizable customer records or imply a concept is a shipped feature.

## Verify and diagnose

Inspect the actual output for legibility, artifacts, faithful UI, contrast, safe areas and rights. Export suitable dimensions/formats and check file size and alt-text needs.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
