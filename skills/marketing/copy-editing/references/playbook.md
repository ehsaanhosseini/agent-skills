# Copy Editing playbook

## Establish the decision

Existing text, audience, purpose, brand voice, approved claims, format and requested editing depth.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Perform focused passes: meaning and factual accuracy, structure and hierarchy, sentence clarity, specificity and proof, consistency, then grammar. Preserve legal qualifiers and product semantics. Remove unsupported superlatives and formulaic filler. Show meaningful edits and flag factual questions instead of silently inventing supporting evidence.

## Worked example

Edit a service page promising guaranteed growth into an evidence-supported statement for owner review; keep all approved contractual terms intact.

## Verify and diagnose

Read the revised piece as a whole, check links/claims/CTA and compare against the original for lost meaning. Explain substantive changes; do not present an unapproved draft as published.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
