# Integration review: deliverability and reply handling

Before drafting a sequence, establish relevance, approved claims, source-backed personalization, recipient context and suppression rules. Separate sending-domain diagnostics from copy quality; do not recommend evasion or domain rotation to bypass anti-abuse controls. Classify replies as interested, question, not now, wrong person, opt-out or hostile; draft respectful responses and stop follow-up on opt-out. Multichannel plans do not authorize any sends or social actions. Never promise a universal response rate.

## Verification

Work from authorized inputs; record observations, assumptions, unverified evidence and the actual checks performed. Compare the proposed outcome with the baseline and preserve unrelated approved behavior. Do not label recommendations or metadata validation as observed product results.
