# Integration review: signal-based qualification

Separate observable account fit from timely demand signals. Build a short research ledger containing source/date, why the signal matters, confidence and the relevant contact role. Prefer company-level qualification and minimal lawful contact data. Suppression and deletion obligations survive enrichment. Public contact visibility is not blanket permission to scrape, enrich or message; retain all existing compliance restrictions. Route drafts to cold-email and lead handoff rules to revops.

## Verification

Work from authorized inputs; record observations, assumptions, unverified evidence and the actual checks performed. Compare the proposed outcome with the baseline and preserve unrelated approved behavior. Do not label recommendations or metadata validation as observed product results.
