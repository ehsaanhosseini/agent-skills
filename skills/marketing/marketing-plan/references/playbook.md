# Marketing Plan playbook

## Establish the decision

Business model, segments, goals, funnel baseline, stage, economics, capacity, budget and constraints.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Diagnose the binding funnel constraint before selecting channels. Map acquisition, activation, retention, referral and revenue to accountable outcomes and baseline evidence. Compare a small set of plausible initiatives using fit, cost, confidence and time to feedback. Sequence a bounded plan with owners and dependencies; do not require an exhaustive audit or a particular tool stack.

## Worked example

For a hotel PMS, distinguish selling software to hotel operators from driving guest bookings. A PMS plan might improve qualified demos, first-property activation and retained paid accounts; a hotel's direct-booking plan uses different economics.

## Verify and diagnose

Show assumptions, budget allocation, milestones, source-backed KPIs, stop/continue criteria and a review date. Keep market-sizing ranges explicit; do not present guessed TAM or historical benchmarks as measured facts.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
