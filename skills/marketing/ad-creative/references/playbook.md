# Ad Creative playbook

## Establish the decision

Approved offer and claims, audience awareness, platform and placement, existing creatives, spend and outcome data.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Build an angle-by-hook matrix from customer evidence. Vary one creative hypothesis at a time: problem, proof, mechanism or objection. Produce complete platform-specific variations with visual direction and a matching destination promise. Separate fatigue from audience saturation and poor landing-page fit; do not infer a winner from CTR alone.

## Worked example

For a clinic booking product, test scheduling convenience against reduced administrative work using approved evidence; never invent patient outcomes or endorsements.

## Verify and diagnose

Check current platform limits, accessibility/captions, licensed assets, claim evidence, destination consistency and a falsifiable test metric. Distinguish concept drafts from rendered assets.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
