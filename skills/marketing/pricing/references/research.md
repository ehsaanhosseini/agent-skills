# Pricing research procedure

Adapted from Marketing Skills pricing/references/research-methods.md at the supplemental source SHA (MIT).

## Van Westendorp

Use the same product description, currency, billing period and segment. Ask threshold prices: (1) too cheap to trust, (2) bargain/good value, (3) expensive but still possible, (4) too expensive to consider. Document exclusions for missing/non-numeric answers and inconsistent ordering (too cheap <= bargain <= expensive <= too expensive); never silently repair responses.

At candidate price p, plot respondent shares with a documented tie convention:

| Curve | Share | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Too cheap | too-cheap threshold >= p | decreasing |
| Cheap | bargain threshold >= p | decreasing |
| Expensive | expensive threshold <= p | increasing |
| Too expensive | too-expensive threshold <= p | increasing |

PMC: Too cheap crosses Expensive. PME: Too expensive crosses Cheap. OPP: Too cheap crosses Too expensive. IDP: Expensive crosses Cheap. PMC–PME is a perceived acceptable range, not a demand/revenue optimum. Four increasing cumulative curves are incorrect. The name OPP does not prove economic optimality.

Worked check: too-cheap thresholds [10,20,30,40] yield share 2/4 at p=25 and 1/4 at p=35. Too-expensive thresholds [40,50,60,70] yield share 2/4 at p=55. Verify directions before intersections; small/discrete samples can yield intervals or unstable intersections.

Choose sample size by coverage and uncertainty, not a universal respondent count. Survey thresholds do not predict conversion/churn; validate with observed behavior or a properly designed purchase/choice study and economic model.

## MaxDiff and packaging

Use balanced best/worst choice sets of relevant features and inspect comprehension, account-level independence and segment differences. Appropriate choice modeling can estimate preference; simple best-minus-worst counts are descriptive, not equivalent utility estimates. Important features may be table stakes or premium differentiators depending on jobs; fixed percentile bands are not automatic packaging rules.

## Rollout

Compare flat/seat/usage/hybrid models with low/median/high usage, included limits, overages and margin sensitivity. Predefine new-customer pilot conversion, net margin, refunds and retention guardrails, observation window and recovery. Existing contracts, billing edits and price publication need appropriate authorization. Report stated preferences separately from measured demand.
