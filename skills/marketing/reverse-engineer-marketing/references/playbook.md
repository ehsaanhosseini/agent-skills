# Reverse Engineer Marketing playbook

## Establish the decision

Target, comparison period, permitted public evidence, own-business constraints, business model and known data gaps.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Build a dated evidence ledger of product, pricing, content and distribution moves. Separate observation, inference and missing data; estimated traffic and coincident events cannot establish causation. Map public funnel steps without entering private systems. Translate plausible mechanisms into small testable plays suited to the user's constraints, not copied claims or designs.

## Worked example

For a hotel technology vendor, compare release dates, public integration announcements and content families; if traffic data is unavailable, give a timeline with uncertainty rather than invented growth rates.

## Verify and diagnose

Cite source/date per material claim, calculate changes only from supplied observed data and show alternative explanations. Preserve industry distinctions such as marketplace liquidity or healthcare privacy.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
