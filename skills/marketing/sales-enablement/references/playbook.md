# Sales Enablement playbook

## Establish the decision

Buyer role, purchase stage, desired next action, validated value, proof, competitive facts and objections.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose the asset around a specific buyer decision. Frame discovery before demonstration; show a realistic buyer workflow and proof, then a clear next step. Answer objections honestly with evidence and limits. Separate observed win/loss reasons from salesperson conjecture and preserve confidentiality.

## Worked example

For a PMS demo, show a reservation-to-operations handoff relevant to the buyer; do not claim a nonexistent integration or expose a real guest's records.

## Verify and diagnose

Check claims against the product, demo environment and pricing terms. Rehearse with synthetic data, failure paths and an alternative to live demos. Track asset usage and stage outcomes without attributing all wins to the asset.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
