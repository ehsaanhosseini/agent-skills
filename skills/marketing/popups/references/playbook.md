# Popups playbook

## Establish the decision

Page intent, audience, existing interruptions, trigger, offer, consent purpose and device context.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose the least interruptive suitable placement. Match trigger to user intent, suppress after conversion/dismissal and avoid stacking prompts. Make any consent request specific and optional; distinguish newsletter capture from cookie choices. Specify keyboard/focus behavior, mobile layout and frequency limits.

## Worked example

An e-commerce offer banner may be better than an immediate fullscreen modal; an exit-intent hypothesis must not hide delivery terms or obstruct checkout.

## Verify and diagnose

Test focus, Escape/dismissal, screen reader labels, small screens, suppressed repeat visits and successful/failed submission. Measure net conversion, bounce and complaints, not captures alone.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
