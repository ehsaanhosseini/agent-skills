# Directory Submissions playbook

## Establish the decision

Product category, audience, verified company details, existing listings, eligibility, cost and approved brand assets.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Select directories for real audience relevance, editorial standards and referral value; do not optimize for domain rating alone. Prepare a consistent entity and category profile, check existing listings to avoid duplication, and track owner, submission status, review requirements and renewal cost. Validate directory legitimacy before any payment or account creation.

## Worked example

For a professional-services portal, prioritize relevant industry associations and customer-used review sites over hundreds of generic startup directories.

## Verify and diagnose

Check published listing identity and destination only after authorized submission. Measure qualified referral sessions, leads and correction needs; link presence does not prove ranking improvement.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
