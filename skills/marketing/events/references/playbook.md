# Events playbook

## Establish the decision

Audience, event objective, format, budget, organizer capacity, accessibility needs, lead consent and sales follow-up.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Choose format based on desired attendee outcome and capacity. Build a timeline for promotion, registration, reminders, rehearsal, live delivery and optional recording. For sponsorships, assess audience quality and total cost, not badge scans alone. Agree lead handling and speaker/content permissions before promotion.

## Worked example

A hotel-operations webinar can offer a practical workflow demonstration, with separate consent for follow-up and a clear route to a product consultation.

## Verify and diagnose

Test registration, timezones, captions, joining links, fallback host and suppression rules. Report registration-to-attendance, qualified meetings, cost per opportunity and delayed pipeline without double-counting existing deals.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
