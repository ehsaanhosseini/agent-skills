# Emails playbook

## Establish the decision

Lifecycle state, explicit consent and suppression rules, triggering event, recipient timezone, frequency limits and desired action.

Use supplied, authorized evidence first. Label missing inputs and choose a reversible recommendation if uncertainty remains; do not invent a baseline, market statistic or guaranteed improvement.

## Method and tradeoffs

Map each message to an event, eligibility condition, delay, exit and one action. Prefer behavior-triggered help to unconditional drips. Coordinate channels to prevent overlapping campaigns; stop on conversion, unsubscribe or changed lifecycle state. Draft subjects, bodies and fallback behavior without deploying flows.

## Worked example

A SaaS trial sequence stops upgrade reminders after payment; an e-commerce cart series stops after an order and excludes unsubscribed contacts.

## Verify and diagnose

Test triggers, race conditions, duplicate webhooks, exit/suppression paths, accessible rendering and links. Evaluate delivered-to-conversion and revenue with holdouts when feasible, not open rate alone.

If evidence is incomplete, state exactly which conclusion cannot be supported and what observation would resolve it. Check for instrumentation faults, cohort selection, seasonality and delayed outcomes before attributing a change to an intervention. Keep unrelated workflows unloaded.

## Deliverable

Provide the artifact requested, its evidence and assumptions, a prioritized recommendation with the material tradeoff, and observable acceptance criteria. Include a staged rollout or recovery plan only when implementation risk requires it. Do not label a proposal as an executed result.
