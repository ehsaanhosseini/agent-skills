# Findings and recheck

Use a finding record: observed element and state; affected task; evidence (screenshot/viewport and brief); impact hypothesis; confidence; proposed change; effort/risk; verification. Severity is high for blocked/unsafe primary tasks, medium for material delay or ambiguity, low for polish with limited impact. Do not assign a single score unless the rubric is calibrated across reviewers.

Example: “At 390 px, the Continue button appears above an unacknowledged pricing error. The user may proceed without seeing why the total changed. Place the error next to the affected line item and require acknowledgment if policy demands it. Verify the same flow at 390 and 1280 px with both error and success fixtures.”

When comparing alternatives, hold data, copy and viewport constant. State what improved and what worsened; do not declare a winner from one screenshot when the task depends on interaction. After implementation, capture the same state, check primary action visibility and information order, and send semantic, contrast or keyboard questions to the appropriate measured audit. Record unresolved assumptions and any unavailable user research.
